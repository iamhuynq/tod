## Audit findings

1. **Archive supports survivorship-free reconstruction.** Delisted symbols remain in
   the archive (e.g. LUNAUSDT, FTTUSDT). 139 candidates stop before the last
   development month. All 47 ranking months have `high` reconstruction confidence;
   0 checksum or download failures.
2. **Timestamp unit change handled.** Archive files from 2025-01 use microseconds;
   the parser normalizes to milliseconds (unit-tested).
3. **Exclusions.** 48 leveraged tokens, 21 stablecoin/fiat bases, 2 detected by peg
   test (BFUSDUSDT, USD1USDT).
4. **High universe churn.** 96 distinct symbols were members at some point. On average
   3.7 of 15 members change each month (max 6). Only 5 symbols are members in every
   month: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP.
5. **New listings enter quickly.** With a 60-day minimum history, recent high-volume
   listings join the top 15 (e.g. 2025-11: FF, MIRA, XPL). Their behavior after
   listing is atypical and may dominate cross-sectional results.
6. **Trading halts appear as gaps.** 9 symbols have missing days (549 total), mostly
   exchange halts (FTT after Nov 2022, CVC, KEY, LUNA). Quality checks in P1 must
   treat these as halts, not data errors.
